How to do it

  1. Kneel on your right knee, left foot forward, bar end in your left hand at shoulder height.
  2. Brace your stomach and squeeze your right glute.
  3. Press the bar up and forward until your arm is long — not straight overhead.
  4. Lower for 2 seconds back to your shoulder.
