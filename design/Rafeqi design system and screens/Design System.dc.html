Status language

Three states, used identically on cards, badges, body maps and charts. Colour is always paired with an icon and a word — never colour alone.

On track

Sage 100 fill · 600 icon · 800 text. Goals met, injury resolved, plan followed.

Needs attention

Terracotta 100 · 500 · 800. Check-in due, recovering injury, a missed session.

Warning

Clay red 100 · 600 · 800. Active injury, pain ≥ 6, health flags, stop-and-see-a-doctor.
